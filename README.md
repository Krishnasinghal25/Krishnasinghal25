### About Me

I'm a data science, AI, and finance enthusiast who loves turning raw numbers into clear, actionable insight. My projects range from machine learning dashboards analyzing employment trends to finance and budgeting tools, backed by solid fundamentals in Java-based systems and algorithm design. I enjoy the full journey from cleaning messy data, to training models, to presenting the results in dashboards that are actually pleasant to use, and I'm always looking for new problems at the intersection of data, intelligence, and money.
